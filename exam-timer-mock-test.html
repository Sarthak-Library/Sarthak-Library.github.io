Why practise with a timer?

Time management decides many exam results. Solving mock tests with a running clock builds speed, shows which sections slow you down and teaches you when to skip and return to a question.

How to take a timed mock test

Check the official duration for your exam, set it in the custom box, put away notes and begin. After the alarm, review every wrong and skipped question and note the reason.
