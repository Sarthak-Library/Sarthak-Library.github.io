FAQ

Questions students ask

Is the library affordable for students on a tight budget?

Yes. Plans start at ₹400 a month, with extra savings on 3 and 6 month plans. We also offer special concessions for deserving students. Please talk to us at the desk or message us on WhatsApp; it stays between us.

Is there an opening offer?

Yes. The first 20 students get ₹100 off every plan: Shift 1 (8 AM to 2 PM) or Shift 2 (2 PM to 8 PM) for ₹400 a month instead of ₹500, or a Full day shift for ₹700 instead of ₹800.

Do you have a free trial?

Yes. We offer 2 free trial days for new students before they decide on a monthly plan.

How much does it cost?

Shift 1 (8 AM to 2 PM) and Shift 2 (2 PM to 8 PM) are ₹400 a month with the opening offer for the first 20 students (regular ₹500). The Full day shift (8 AM to 8 PM) is ₹700 a month (regular ₹800). Booking 3 months saves 10% and booking 6 months saves 15%.

Which exams do students prepare for here?

Students preparing for UPSC and State PCS, SSC, Defence (NDA, CDS, Agniveer), Police and Banking exams study here, along with college students. It is a self-study space where you study independently with your own material.

Is there CCTV and security?

Yes. The library has CCTV coverage, and the space is built for quiet, safe study habits.

Do I get my own seat?

Yes. Every member gets a personal seat in the shift they choose. We assign your seat when we confirm your booking, and you can see it in the student portal.

What are your timings?

We are open every day from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Shift 1 runs 8 AM to 2 PM, Shift 2 runs 2 PM to 8 PM, and the Full day shift covers both.

Where is the library?

355, Moh. Gangapuram Colony, Tilhar Zai, near Malik Montessori School and Loharon Wala Chauraha, Shahjahanpur. Walk in any day for a free look around.

How do I reserve a seat?

Use the booking form on the Plans & fees page: pick a plan and a start date, then send your request on WhatsApp. We will call you to confirm. You can also call +91 94508 60232.

How can students check their seat and fees?

Members can log in to the student portal on this website with their Member ID and password to see their seat, fees paid and due, and payments, and to update their details.

Still have a question?

Walk in for a free look around, or message us and we will reply on WhatsApp.
