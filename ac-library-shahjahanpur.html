A comfortable place for independent study

Long study sessions can be demanding, and a comfortable environment can help students settle into a regular routine. Sarthak Library and Self Study Zone is an air-conditioned self-study library in Shahjahanpur for students who want a dedicated place to read, revise, practise questions and prepare for examinations.

Whether you are preparing for SSC, banking, defence recruitment, UPSC, State PCS or college examinations, you can bring your own books, notes and study material and follow your personal timetable. The library provides a study space; it does not claim to provide coaching or guarantee examination results.

Facilities for focused study

The library offers an air-conditioned study environment along with personal seating, 100 Mbps Wi-Fi, RO drinking water and CCTV surveillance. These facilities are intended to make daily study more convenient while maintaining a quiet and disciplined atmosphere for members.

Use your seat for reading, note-making, online study resources and practice papers. Please respect other students by keeping your phone silent and taking calls outside the study area.

Daily timings and study options

Sarthak Library is open every day from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. The study options include Shift 1 from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Shift 2 from 2:00 PM to 8:00 PM, and a full-day option from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Published monthly plans start at ₹400. Contact the library to confirm current prices, available seats and the terms of the advertised two-day free trial before visiting.
