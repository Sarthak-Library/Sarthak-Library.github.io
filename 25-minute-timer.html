Why 25 minutes?

Twenty-five minutes is the classic Pomodoro length. It is short enough to start without dread and long enough to make real progress on a topic, a chapter or a set of questions.

What to do in 25 minutes

Read and summarise a chapter section, solve a set of practice questions, revise formulas or write answers. After the alarm, rest for 5 minutes, stretch and drink water before the next round.
