How to use this online timer

Tap a preset such as 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, or type any number of minutes in the custom box and press Set. Press Start to begin, Pause to stop for a moment and Reset to go back to the full time. An alarm beep plays when the countdown reaches zero.

Good uses for a countdown timer

Students use a countdown timer for timed practice sets, revision blocks, break reminders and speed drills. It is also handy for cooking, group activities, presentations and short exercise rounds.

Does the timer work when the screen is locked?
