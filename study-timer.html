Plan your study blocks

Divide your day into blocks of 45 to 90 minutes for hard subjects and 25 minutes for lighter revision. End each block with 5 to 10 minutes of rest. Write down the target before you press Start, for example 'finish 30 reasoning questions'.

Tips to stay focused

Keep only the books you need on your desk, silence notifications and decide what you will do in the block before starting. Review mistakes at the end of each session instead of during it.
