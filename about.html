About Sarthak Library and Self Study Zone

Who we are

Sarthak Library and Self Study Zone is a quiet self-study library in Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh. It is a calm, disciplined space for students preparing for competitive exams, government jobs and college studies.

Who studies here

Students preparing for UPSC and State PCS, SSC, Defence (NDA, CDS, Agniveer), Police and Banking exams study here, along with college students. Members study independently with their own material.

What members get

A personal seat in the chosen shift, 100 Mbps Wi-Fi, RO drinking water, CCTV coverage and a strict silence rule. See the facilities and plans and fees.

Library rules

Keep your phone on silent and take calls outside, speak softly, sit only on your own seat and in your own shift, and keep your desk and the floor clean. Loud music and group study are not allowed.

Opening hours

Open every day from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Shift 1 runs 8 AM to 2 PM, Shift 2 runs 2 PM to 8 PM, and the Full day shift covers both.
