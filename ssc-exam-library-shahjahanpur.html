Create a steady SSC preparation routine

SSC examinations such as CGL, CHSL, MTS and other recruitment tests require regular practice and revision. Aspirants often need time for arithmetic, advanced mathematics, reasoning, English comprehension, vocabulary and general awareness. A consistent study space can make it easier to protect time for these subjects and reduce interruptions. Sarthak Library and Self Study Zone provides a dedicated self-study environment in Shahjahanpur. It is for independent learners who bring their own books, notes, question banks and online resources. The library is not an SSC coaching centre and does not promise exam results; it offers a place to study according to your own plan.

Plan sessions around the SSC syllabus

You can divide your study hours into subject blocks. For example, use one session for concepts and examples, another for timed question practice, and a final short session for reviewing mistakes. Keep a record of accuracy and time taken in mock tests. Use official SSC notifications and exam materials to confirm the latest syllabus, dates and eligibility requirements, since these can change.

Facilities and study environment

The library lists personal seating, 100 Mbps Wi-Fi, RO drinking water and CCTV surveillance among its facilities. A quiet-zone environment is intended to help members concentrate and respect one another's study time. Bring your own study materials and follow the library's rules, including keeping your phone silent and taking calls outside.
