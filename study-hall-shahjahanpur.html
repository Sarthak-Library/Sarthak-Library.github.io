A study hall for independent learning

A good study hall should make it easier to concentrate, organise your time and return to your work each day. Sarthak Library and Self Study Zone is a self-study space in Shahjahanpur for students who want a dedicated place outside home to read, revise, prepare for examinations or work on college subjects. Whether you are preparing for a government recruitment exam or keeping up with college coursework, you can bring your own books and notes and follow your own study plan. The library provides the study space; it does not claim to provide teaching, tutoring or guaranteed academic outcomes.

Personal seating and a quiet atmosphere

The library lists personal seating, 100 Mbps Wi-Fi, RO drinking water and CCTV surveillance. Members are expected to maintain a quiet, respectful environment, keep phones on silent and take calls outside. These shared habits help make the hall more comfortable for people working on different subjects and examination goals.

Choose a study shift

The library is open every day from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. The available shift options are Shift 1 from 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Shift 2 from 2:00 PM to 8:00 PM and a full-day option from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Published monthly plans start at ₹400. Contact the team for current prices, seat availability and details of the advertised two-day free trial before visiting.
