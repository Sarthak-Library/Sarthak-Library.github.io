Quick answers

Questions students ask before joining

What is Sarthak Library?

Sarthak Library is a quiet self-study library in Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh. It offers 50 personal seats, 100 Mbps Wi-Fi, RO drinking water, CCTV and a strict silence rule for students preparing for competitive and college exams. The library is open every day from 8 AM to 8 PM.

What is a self-study library?

A self-study library is a quiet study space where students bring their own books and notes and prepare independently, without classes or coaching. Members get a personal seat, fast Wi-Fi and a silent room, so they can focus on their own exam syllabus for long hours every day.

How much does the library cost per month?

Shift 1 (8 AM to 2 PM) and Shift 2 (2 PM to 8 PM) cost ₹400 a month with the opening offer for the first 20 students, instead of ₹500. The Full day shift (8 AM to 8 PM) costs ₹700 instead of ₹800. Booking 3 months saves 10% and 6 months saves 15%.

What is the opening offer?

The opening offer is a ₹100 monthly discount on every plan for the first 20 students who join. It brings Shift 1 and Shift 2 down to ₹400 a month and the Full day shift down to ₹700 a month. Regular prices apply after the first 20 students.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New students get 2 free trial days before choosing a monthly plan, so you can check the silence, Wi-Fi and study environment for yourself. Walk in any day between 8 AM and 8 PM, or call +91 94508 60232 to arrange your trial in advance.

What is a shift?

A shift is a fixed daily time block for your personal seat. Shift 1 runs from 8 AM to 2 PM, Shift 2 runs from 2 PM to 8 PM, and the Full day shift covers both from 8 AM to 8 PM. You study in the shift you choose when you book.

Where is Sarthak Library located?

Sarthak Library is at 355, Moh. Gangapuram Colony, Tilhar Zai, near Malik Montessori School and Loharon Wala Chauraha, Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh 242001. It is open every day from 8 AM to 8 PM, and you can walk in any day for a free look around.

How do I reserve a seat?

Open the booking form on the Plans & fees page, pick a plan and a start date, then send your request on WhatsApp. The library team calls you to confirm and assigns your personal seat. You can also call or message +91 94508 60232 to reserve.

Which exams can I prepare for here?

Students preparing for UPSC and State PCS, SSC, Defence (NDA, CDS, Agniveer), Police and Banking exams study here, along with college students. It is a self-study space, so you study independently with your own books and notes in a strictly silent room. See all FAQs.

How to reserve a seat in 3 steps

  1. Open the booking form and pick a plan and a start date.
  2. Enter your name and mobile number and send the request on WhatsApp.
  3. We call you to confirm and assign your seat.
