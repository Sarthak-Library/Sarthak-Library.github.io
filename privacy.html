Privacy

Privacy policy

Booking requests

The booking form on the Plans & fees page builds a WhatsApp message with the name, mobile number and plan details you type. When you press Send WhatsApp, the same details are also saved to the library’s database (Supabase) so the team can see your request and confirm your seat. The library team uses them only for that purpose.

Student portal

Members log in with a Member ID and password to see their seat, fees and payments, and to update their father’s name, address, alternate mobile and PIN code. These details are stored in a database service (Supabase) that the library uses to manage memberships. Members may also upload a PDF copy of their Aadhaar when they register. It is kept securely in a private storage area that only the library administrator can open, and it is used only for identity verification and administrative purposes. It is deleted or securely disposed of when no longer needed for these purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements. See our Terms for more.

Cookies, analytics and ads

This website has no advertising and no analytics trackers. It saves only your light or dark theme choice in your browser’s local storage. The free study timers run entirely in your browser and send nothing to the library. Nothing about your visit is sold or shared for advertising.

Third-party services

Page fonts load from Google Fonts. Map links open Google Maps and WhatsApp links open WhatsApp only when you choose them. Those services have their own privacy policies and may receive your device information when you use them. Please read their policies if you want to know more.

Your choices
