Terms

Terms of use and membership

Sarthak Library and Self Study Zone, Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh. Last updated: 27 November 2026. These terms apply when you visit our website, reserve a seat or study at Sarthak Library. By reserving a seat or joining, you agree to them.

1. About us

Sarthak Library and Self Study Zone is a quiet self-study space at 355, Moh. Gangapuram Colony, Tilhar Zai, Near Malik Montessori School, Loharon Wala Chauraha, Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh 242001. Phone and WhatsApp: +91 94508 60232.

2. Timings and shifts

  • The library is open every day from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
  • Shift 1 runs 8:00 AM to 2:00 PM, Shift 2 runs 2:00 PM to 8:00 PM, and Full day covers both.
  • You may use your seat only during the shift you have paid for.

3. Reserving a seat

  • Reserving online or on WhatsApp is a request. Your seat is confirmed only when we confirm it to you.
  • There are 50 seats. If all seats are taken, you may be put on a waiting list.
  • A seat is for one named student and cannot be shared, sold or passed to someone else.
  • We may change a seat number if needed, with notice to you.
  • New students may take a 2-day free trial, subject to seat availability.

4. Fees, payment and refunds

  • There is no joining fee.
  • Reservation fee: a ₹100 reservation fee is required to reserve a seat. It is adjusted against your membership fee when you join.
  • Trial refund: if you decide not to continue, the reservation fee is fully refundable during the 2-day trial period.
  • After you join: once the trial is over and your membership is confirmed, fees paid for the ongoing membership period are non-refundable. The library management may consider exceptional cases at its discretion.
  • Fees are listed on the Plans & fees page. Offers apply only to the students and period stated there. Longer plans may carry the discounts shown on that page.
  • Due date and grace period: membership fees must be paid by the due date. You get a grace period of 3 days. If payment is not received within this period, your seat may be released and given to another student.
  • The library keeps your payment records, and you can ask to see your payment entries at any time.

5. Closed days

If the library itself closes for any reason, we will extend your membership by the same number of days. This extension does not apply to holidays or absences you choose to take, unless the library management announces otherwise.

6. Rules inside the library

  • Silence is strictly maintained. Take calls outside the study room and keep phones on silent.
  • Keep your desk and the premises clean. Do not damage furniture, Wi-Fi equipment or other property.
  • Respect other students and staff. Harassment, fighting or any behaviour that disturbs others is not allowed.
  • Smoking, tobacco, alcohol and illegal substances are not allowed on the premises.
  • Wi-Fi is for study use. Illegal, harmful or inappropriate use is not allowed.
  • We may ask a member to leave, or end a membership, for repeated or serious rule breaking. Fees paid for the ongoing period are non-refundable in that case.

7. Your belongings

Please look after your own books, bags, phones and valuables. CCTV is installed for safety, but the library is not responsible for lost, stolen or damaged belongings.

8. Student portal and your account

  • Members log in with a Member ID and a password they set themselves. Keep your password private. You are responsible for activity under your login.
  • Activation codes expire after 7 days. Ask us for a new one if yours has expired.
  • After several wrong password attempts, the account locks for a short time to protect it.
  • Please keep the details you give us (name, mobile, father’s name, address, PIN) correct and up to date.
  • Do not try to access other members’ information or the admin area. Attempting to break into or misuse the website or portal is not permitted and may be reported to the authorities.

9. Aadhaar and personal information

  • We may ask for a PDF copy of your Aadhaar when you register. You tick a consent box when you upload it.
  • Aadhaar copies are kept securely in a private storage area that only the library administrator can open. They are used only for identity verification and administrative purposes. We do not publish or sell them.
  • They are deleted or securely disposed of when no longer needed for these purposes, subject to applicable legal requirements.
  • How we handle all personal information is explained in our Privacy Policy.

10. The website and free tools

  • We try to keep the website accurate, but timings, offers, prices and seat availability can change. Please confirm with us before paying.
  • The free study timers are provided as they are, without any guarantee.
  • The website, logo, text and images belong to Sarthak Library. Please do not copy or reuse them without permission.

11. Our responsibility

We provide a study space, not coaching, and we do not guarantee any exam result. To the extent the law allows, the library is not responsible for losses caused by events outside our control, such as power cuts, internet outages or closures for safety reasons.

12. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use the library after a change means you accept it.

13. Governing law and contact

These terms are governed by the laws of India. Any dispute will be handled in the courts at Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh.

Questions? Call or WhatsApp +91 94508 60232, or visit us at the address above.
