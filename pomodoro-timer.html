What is the Pomodoro technique?

The Pomodoro technique splits work into short, focused intervals, traditionally 25 minutes, separated by 5 minute breaks. After four focus sessions you take a longer break of 15 to 30 minutes. Many students find that a fixed time limit makes it easier to start difficult chapters and to avoid checking the phone.

How to use this Pomodoro timer for exam preparation

Pick one topic or one set of questions, press Start and work until the beep. Take the 5 minute break away from your desk, then start the next session. Use sessions for reading, practice questions, mock test review or revision notes. Keep a small tally of completed sessions so you can see your daily study hours.

How long is a Pomodoro?

A standard Pomodoro is 25 minutes of focus followed by a 5 minute short break. After four Pomodoros, take a longer break.
